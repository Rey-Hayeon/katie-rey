Q. Hongdae or Myeongdong, where should I stay?
→ Hongdae: young, artsy, lots of cafes and nightlife. The airport train (AREX) goes straight to Hongik Univ. Station.
→ Myeongdong: central, great for shopping and street food, easy to get around the city.
→ Katie's pick: [Katie]

Q. How do locals get around?
→ Subway and bus! Get a T-money card at any convenience store and top it up with cash.
→ Google Maps doesn't work well for directions in Korea. Use Naver Map or Kakao Map instead.

Q. What's the weather like? What should I pack?
→ Spring (Mar–May) & Fall (Sep–Nov): mild, bring layers.
→ Summer (Jun–Aug): hot and humid, with a rainy season around July.
→ Winter (Dec–Feb): really cold, often below 0°C. Bring a warm padded jacket!
→ Katie's tip: [Katie]

→ KakaoTalk (you have it now! 🎉), Naver Map or Kakao Map, Papago (translator)
→ More from me: [Katie]
